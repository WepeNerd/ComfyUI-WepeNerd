The idea is simple

An image edit becomes a video edit.

  1. 01

    Choose your moment

    Load a video, choose the shot, and place anchor markers on the frames you want to edit. Adjust the crop and output size in the same view.

  2. 02

    Make the change

    Edit the marked frames in your preferred image editor, or connect a local model. Use a second anchor when another view needs the same change.

  3. 03

    Let it ripple

    H3 uses the source video and edited anchors to propagate the change through the shot. Save the result and a side-by-side comparison.

You’ll need a current ComfyUI installation and hardware capable of running MiniMax H3. The local editor also runs Qwen Image 2.1.

Full installation guide ↗
01

Install the custom nodes

Clone ComfyUI-WepeNerd into ComfyUI/custom_nodes, install its requirements with ComfyUI’s Python environment, then restart ComfyUI.

02

Get the models

Open the workflow’s Models & setup card for exact downloads and folders. Both workflows need H3 FL2VA, its text encoder and video VAE, the Turbo LoRA, and the Ripple LoRA.

Local Editor adds Qwen Image 2.1, its text encoder and VAE. Both image-editing branches share those three files.

03

Load your video and edit

Select your own video in Ripple Clip. Choose the shot, output size and anchors. In Simple, upload your edited frame; in Local Editor, replace both example edit instructions with your intended change.

Ripple Output is enabled in these downloads, so queuing also renders the H3 video. Start with a small output size and keep your anchor edits consistent.
